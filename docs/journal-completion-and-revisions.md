# Journal completion and later edits

- Drafts autosave. First submission marks the entry completed.
- The initial journal EcoPoints award is an immutable ledger snapshot. Editing,
  reopening, saving, or pressing the final action again does not repeat it.
- The completion popup is gated by first publication and an atomic, one-time
  journal reward notice claim. Initial eligible impact awards are included.
- Completed entries remain editable and autosave their updates. Their final
  action says Done; submitting again is not required to retain edits.
- Implementation autosave preserves its submitted marker.

Any future reward for a revision needs a separate, explicit product rule; it
must not replay the original completion award. Existing impact deduplication
and per-entry caps remain in force.
