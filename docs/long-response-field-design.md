# Text field sizing

Explanations, descriptions, reflections, methods, outcomes, notes, instructions,
questions, objectives, and other prose prompts start with five visible lines.
Use `AppMetrics.longResponseLines`, top-aligned text, and natural vertical growth
where the page scrolls. Existing document canvases may retain larger editor areas.
Do not force a growing input into a short fixed-height parent.

Titles, names, search, numbers, dates, URLs, table labels, matching terms, and
explicitly short answers retain compact sizing. Short media captions may retain
their smaller multiline controls.

Maintain the established label/helper gaps, content edges, and centered actions.
